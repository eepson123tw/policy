Privacy Policy

Smart Translator Chrome Extension

Last updated: January 12, 2025

Your privacy matters to us. In this policy, we explain:

  • What data we collect and why.
  • How we handle your data.
  • Your rights regarding your data.

We pledge never to sell your data—not now, not ever.

This policy applies to the Smart Translator Chrome Extension (hereinafter referred to as "the Extension"). This document outlines how we collect, process, and protect information from users of the Extension.

What We Collect and Why

1. Identity & Access

When you configure the Extension, we may collect:

  • Your email address, if provided for troubleshooting or support purposes.
  • API keys for translation services (e.g., OpenAI API Key), solely to enable the Extension’s functionality. These keys are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

2. Browser Content Access

To perform its primary function of translating text and images, the Extension may:

  • Access selected text on web pages to provide translations.
  • Capture visible portions of the screen for image-based translations (after explicit user action, such as clicking "Capture and Translate").

3. Notifications

We use browser notifications to:

  • Alert you about the status of translation tasks (e.g., success or errors).
  • Provide configuration or usage tips.

4. Storage

The Extension uses browser storage to save:

  • Your API keys and preferred language settings.
  • Recently translated content for convenience.

When We Access or Disclose Your Information

The Extension processes your data locally and does not transmit personal or browsing data to third parties, except:

  • When you explicitly use a third-party translation API (e.g., OpenAI API), your data is sent directly to the respective service.

Legal Requirements: We will disclose data only if legally required, such as in response to valid legal processes or subpoenas.

Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Access: View the data stored by the Extension, including settings and recent translations.
  • Correction: Update or correct API keys and preferences directly within the Extension settings.
  • Deletion: Remove all stored data by resetting the Extension.

We do not store any data on our servers, so there is no centralized repository of your information. Any retained data resides solely on your device.

Data Retention

The Extension retains data only as long as necessary to perform its core functions. For example:

  • API keys and preferences are stored until you manually remove them.
  • Translation tasks use temporary data that is discarded immediately after processing.

Cookies and Tracking

The Extension does not use cookies or tracking technologies to monitor your browsing activity. However, third-party translation services you configure may use their own tracking mechanisms as outlined in their respective privacy policies.

Location of Data

All data processed by the Extension remains on your device or is transmitted directly to the translation API you configure. No data is sent to or stored on our servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Significant changes will be communicated via the Extension's update notes.
